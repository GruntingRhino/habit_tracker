# Hook Library

## Self-recognition hooks
- Every Monday, I start over.
- I keep restarting my habits every Monday.
- I know what to do. I just do not do it.
- I tried five productivity apps and used none.
- Skipping one day turns into skipping a week.

## Objection hooks
- You do not need another complicated productivity app.
- You do not need more motivation.
- You need a system that survives Tuesday.
- You do not need five habits. You need one.
- The problem is not discipline. The problem is friction.

## Challenge hooks
- Pick one habit for 7 days.
- Track one habit and stop restarting.
- Set your first reminder today.
- Make consistency visible.
- Start with one habit today.

## Contrast hooks
- Before: restart every Monday. After: visible consistency.
- Before: random effort. After: one system.
- Before: five apps. After: one habit.
- Before: motivation. After: reminders and check-offs.
- Before: guessing. After: visible progress.

## Creator-style lines
- I stopped trying to do everything at once.
- I only needed one habit I would actually keep.
- The fix was not another app.
- The fix was making the next step obvious.
- The win is small. The signal matters.
