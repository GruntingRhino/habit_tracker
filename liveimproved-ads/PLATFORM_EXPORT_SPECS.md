# Platform Export Specs

## Shared baseline
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- Captions: burned in, high contrast, large enough for mobile
- Pace: hook by 0.5s, product/problem by 2s
- Audio: voiceover optional, captions mandatory
- Finish: CTA in the last 2 seconds

## TikTok
- Best lengths: 7s, 12s, 15s
- Style: rougher, creator-native, less polished
- Strongest use: self-recognition hooks and challenge framing
- Avoid: corporate intro cards and long logo reveals

## Instagram Reels
- Best lengths: 12s, 15s, 24s
- Style: UGC with slightly cleaner typography
- Strongest use: objection crushers and challenge offers
- Avoid: dense copy blocks

## YouTube Shorts
- Best lengths: 15s, 24s
- Style: slightly more explanatory, but still fast
- Strongest use: demos and proof-based explanations
- Avoid: slow buildup

## Meta feed / stories
- Best lengths: 10s, 12s, 15s
- Style: direct-response, high-contrast, clean hierarchy
- Strongest use: retargeting and activation CTAs
- Avoid: over-editing that makes it feel like a brand film

## Safe-area guidance
- keep critical text away from top/bottom UI bars
- do not place the CTA too low on Stories
- reserve the center for the hook and the proof frame

## Export naming
- `platform_funnel_hook_duration_v1.mp4`
- examples: `tiktok_cold_monday-reset-7s_v1.mp4`, `reels_warm_one-habit-15s_v2.mp4`

## Deliverables per ad
- final MP4
- caption copy
- thumbnail / first frame
- CTA variant list
- real UI source list
